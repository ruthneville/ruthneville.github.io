About

I am a spatial data scientist and population geographer, currently a Research Fellow at the Bartlett Centre for Advanced Spatial Analysis (CASA), University College London.

Background

My research focuses on migration, mobility and housing, and draws on spatial analysis, statistical modelling and computational methods.

I completed my PhD in Geographic Data Science at the University of Liverpool in 2025. My thesis, Drivers, Disruptions, and Future Trajectories: Understanding International Student Mobility to the UK, examined the factors shaping international student mobility, the effects of Brexit and COVID-19, and approaches to forecasting future flows.

Before my PhD, I completed an MSc in Geographic Data Science at Liverpool, an MA in Political Science at the University of Manchester, and a BA in Philosophy, Politics and Economics at Lancaster University.

Teaching and academic service

I teach GIS and spatial analysis at UCL, including R-based methods and Master's dissertation supervision. I have also delivered guest teaching in spatial analysis for public health at the University of Oxford, and previously taught geographic information systems and quantitative methods at the University of Liverpool. I am an Associate Fellow of Advance HE.

My academic service includes work with the Royal Geographical Society's Quantitative Methods Research Group and co-editing a special section of The Geographical Journal on mobilising spatial data for spatial populations.
